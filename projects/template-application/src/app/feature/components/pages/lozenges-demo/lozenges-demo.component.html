Lozenges

Compact status indicators. Semantic colors track the app's light/dark theme; accent colors are fixed brand tones. The interactive trigger pairs a lozenge with a menu for inline status editing.

Semantic appearances

Use these to communicate state. They derive from the shared status palette and adapt to the active theme.
